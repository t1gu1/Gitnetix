## What Gitnetix does to your projects

Gitnetix acts on your projects with your own permissions. Among other things, it can:

- run Git commands that rewrite or remove work, such as rebase, reset, merge, force push and branch delete
- create, move and delete branches, worktrees, stashes and files
- start AI agents, such as Claude Code, Codex, Copilot, Antigravity or Ollama models, that read, edit and delete files and run commands on your computer
- sign in to Git hosts and ticket trackers, such as GitHub, GitLab, Jira or Linear, and create, change or close pull requests, tickets and releases there with your account
- open a remote access server so you can use Gitnetix from your phone

Any of these can lose work or cause harm if something goes wrong. The cause can be a bug in Gitnetix, a mistake by an AI agent, a wrong setting, or a problem in a tool or service Gitnetix uses.
