v0.22.0

21 Nov 2026

Features

  • Ticket reader. Open any ticket in a focus reader with its work line, status rings and linked pull request. ACME-142
  • Markdown diff preview. Added text is tinted and removed text is struck through when you preview a Markdown file.
  • Map legend. The legend fits on one line at the bottom of the map, and See all opens every symbol.

Fixes

  • Branches whose refs did not move no longer show stale ahead and behind counts after a fetch. ACME-138
  • A push that loses its connection retries once instead of failing the whole release. ACME-140
  • The legend box keeps its margin beside the side panel.

Internal

  • Updated Tauri to 2.9 and Vite to 7.2. ACME-131
